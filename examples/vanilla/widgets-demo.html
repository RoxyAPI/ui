Auto-mounted widgets

Each block below is a single <div data-roxy-widget="...">. The one-tag script reads its data-* attributes and mounts the matching component, fetching from the browser with a publishable key.

Mint a publishable key at roxyapi.com/account, then replace the pk_live_replace_with_your_key placeholder below with your own.

Loaded card: sign supplied

The tag carries data-sign, so it fetches on mount and renders the reading.

Interactive widget: no sign supplied

The same tag without data-sign renders a 12-sign tile picker, and fetches when a sign is tapped.

Birth-data widget: natal chart

A chart needs a date, a time, and a place, so it renders a styled form with a city search and draws the wheel on submit.
